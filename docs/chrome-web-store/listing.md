## Description

Spark is the companion inside ShuaCrew, the Mac app for working with your AI crew. This extension brings Spark to every web page.

**Highlight anything, then pick what Spark does**
• Explain: plain words, no jargon
• Summarize: the point, in a few lines
• Rewrite: clearer, shorter, or in your tone
• Reply: a draft answer to what you selected
• Ask: any question about it
• Save: straight into your ShuaCrew Library, with the page link
• Hand to the crew: turn it into a task for your agents

Right-click a selection, or press ⌥⇧S (Alt+Shift+S). Right-click the page itself to summarize the whole thing, or let Spark offer when you open a long read. That offer can be turned off in the popup.

**Exact on web pages**
When you ask Spark on your Mac "where's the export button?" or "fill in my email", Spark uses the extension to find the actual control on the page: its real name and its real shape. It points at the exact thing, or clicks and types for you when you asked it to. No guessing from a screenshot.

**Private by design**
Spark talks only to ShuaCrew running on your own Mac (127.0.0.1). No accounts, no analytics, no servers of ours. Answers come from your own Claude subscription through ShuaCrew.

**Requires** ShuaCrew on macOS. Pair once: open ShuaCrew → Settings → Shua companion → Spark for Chrome, copy the key, and paste it into the Spark popup.
